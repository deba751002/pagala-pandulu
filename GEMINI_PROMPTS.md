# Gemini prompts — Pagala ♥ Pandulu portfolio

## How to use
1. Open a new Gemini chat and upload 3–5 clear photos of the two of you (faces visible, good light).
2. Paste **STEP 1** first. Then paste one prompt from **STEP 2** at a time. Make one image per message.
3. Download each image and save it with the file name shown next to it, inside `couple-portfolio/images/`.
4. For the images marked **(cut-out)**: remove the background with remove.bg or your phone, and save as PNG.

---

## STEP 1 — paste this first (sets the style for every image)

```
I am making a cinematic love-story website for me and my partner. I have uploaded our photos.
For every image I ask for in this chat, follow these rules:

1. Use the exact faces, hairstyles and body types of the two people in my uploaded photos. They must be clearly recognisable. Do not change their faces, skin tone or age.
2. Style: cinematic film still, moody and romantic, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, soft film grain.
3. Colour grade: dark background with warm red, orange and golden highlights (like a sunset or firelight). Deep shadows, glowing warm light, no neon blue or green.
4. Clothing: stylish and elegant, dark or neutral colours (black, cream, deep red) so it matches the red-and-gold theme.
5. Photorealistic. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no logos, no extra people unless I ask.
6. Always use the exact aspect ratio I give.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first image request.
```
